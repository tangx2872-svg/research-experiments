# Paper limitations (factual material, ready for the limitation section)

## L1 — Doll stable regression
Doll is the only category with a stable negative ΔAUROC (3-seed mean −0.03850, 0/3 positive seeds, paired-image bootstrap CI [−0.0661, −0.0140], bootstrap positive-difference proportion P(ΔAUROC>0) = 0.001, which is a one-sided resampling proportion and NOT a two-sided p-value). The audit in `results/e23/` shows the failure is present in all three bank seeds, broad across illumination groups (8–9 of 10 groups negative per seed), and consistent with a mild near-symmetric score shift (the anomaly-side relative drop is 0.95–1.00 of the normal-side drop) rather than an anomaly-information collapse. No causal mechanism is established; the low-baseline/high-headroom explanation does not apply (Doll has the lowest F0 baseline and the lowest d′).

## L2 — Multi-view heterogeneity (E21)
E21 (4 views × Bird/Car/Cube × seed0) reports 8/12 positive cells with a macro pooled ΔAUROC of +0.00142 (stratified bootstrap CI [−0.0032, +0.0062]) and ΔAP of −0.00165, and it is a HOLD: the benefit is not restricted to view120 but is highly heterogeneous, with Car at views 240/330 and Cube at views 090/330 being negative (worst: Car v330 ΔAUROC −0.02600). Seed1/2 were not run for E21.

## L3 — Evaluation scope
The complete 10-category validation is on M²AD (view120, 300-bank, official `image_anomaly` + `detectable != False`) with a single backbone (DINOv2-B). MVTec AD 2 external validation was audited but is a HOLD (needs manual dataset access; single-scene ΔAUROC noise half-width 0.04–0.07). Cross-dataset generalization was untested before E25; E25 now supplies one conditional external evaluation (see L6), so the limitation is narrowed rather than removed.

## L4 — Shift–gain association is exploratory
The illumination-shift ↔ gain association has n = 10 categories (Pearson r = +0.371, p = 0.291; Spearman ρ = +0.576, p = 0.082) and is reported as association only. Doll is an outlier of this weak trend (residual −0.0453 against the n = 10 fit); a leave-one-out sensitivity excluding Doll raises the Pearson r to +0.807 (p = 0.008) but is reported only alongside the full n = 10 result.

## L5 — Two different uncertainties
Per-category paired-image bootstrap CIs quantify test-set sampling uncertainty only; cross-seed variability is reported separately as the std over the 3 bank seeds (e.g. Doll ΔAUROC −0.03850 ± 0.004638, sample std with ddof=1 over the 3 bank seeds). The two are never merged, and image bootstrap CIs must not be read as cross-seed uncertainty.

## L6 — External evaluation is conditional and re-trained (E25)
E25 evaluates R1-ILA on RobustAD (CC-BY-4.0), a dataset independent of M²AD. The adapter is re-trained from scratch on each RobustAD sub-dataset's own 120 source-train normals (hyperparameters read only from `results/e17/final_method.json`; 3 adapter seeds), and one adapter per (sub-dataset, seed) serves all of that sub-dataset's target domains; both variants score the same 180-normal bank on the same target splits. This is therefore external dataset validation with re-training, NOT a direct transfer of the M²AD adapter. Cell accounting: 13 splits × 3 seeds = 39 completed cells, of which 33 (11 valid target splits × 3 seeds) enter the target aggregates, 3 are PCB test0 (source reference) and 3 are MetalParts test0, which is excluded under the original protocol (145 file names shared with source-train) and is reported with valid=0 but never zero-filled. A 2026-10-19 byte-level SHA-256 audit of that overlap found 0 of 145 pairs byte-identical (`results/e25/overlap_audit.json`), so exact byte duplication is excluded but near-duplication was NOT tested: the "same stored files" rationale is superseded and the exclusion is retained conservatively, with a weakened rather than proven rationale. That validity rule is file-name-level and outcome-independent; it is documented before the MetalParts run but, because protocol and results were committed together, it cannot be proven pre-registered. The result is sub-dataset dependent: PCB +0.01835 (5/5 splits positive in all 3 seeds) and MetalParts −0.01725 (2/6 splits positive, background and scale negative in all 3 seeds). The overall macro ΔAUROC is −0.001068 with split-level weighting and +0.000550 with sub-dataset weighting (full-precision inputs in both cases), so the sign depends on the weighting and no overall improvement is claimed; the descriptive t-interval over the 3 per-seed macros is [−0.0033, +0.0012]. Coverage limits: 2 of 3 RobustAD sub-datasets (PiledBags not downloaded), a single backbone, plain image-level AUROC/AP (RobustAD has no `detectable` field), and anisotropic 448×448 resizing for MetalParts.
